# About

*OVERRAIDE* is an experimental short film. This weblog documents how it is being made: the proposal, technical tests, film reviews, reading notes, shoot days and a final reflection. It was made for CIN506 Experimental Film and Animation at Ulster University.

Tools and AI use: planning conversations with an AI assistant (Claude) helped organise research leads, shot lists and the small software tools used in production. The footage, the edit and the reflection are my own. Every reference is checked against its source.

<p class="meta">All metrics, interfaces and data shown in the film and on this site are fabricated. No real clients, chats or analytics appear.</p>
