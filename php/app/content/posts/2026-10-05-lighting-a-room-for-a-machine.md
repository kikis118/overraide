Planning the Gaussian splat capture of the empty room (Kerbl et al., 2023). Splats fail in the dark: noise and motion blur break the camera solve. So the capture will be lit as brightly and evenly as possible, with the darkness added back in the grade.

That contrast is useful. My own footage is dark and screen-lit. The machine's view of the room can stay evenly lit and slightly too clean, with floaters at the edges. The machine doesn't need the dark to see.
