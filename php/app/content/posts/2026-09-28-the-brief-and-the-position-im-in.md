Read the brief and the rubric. The top band asks for work that is both *artisanal* and *personal*, so polished After Effects work on its own risks reading as derivative technique.

First idea: my job itself. I edit for two creators who are, loosely, competitors. I sit in both group chats and quietly decide what their audiences see. That position, an invisible hand with real influence, is the most personal thing I have to make a film about.

Early references to follow up: the Kuleshov experiment (the cut creates meaning), Vertov showing his own editor on screen, Warhol's two side-by-side projections in *Chelsea Girls*, and Martin Arnold looping a few seconds of found footage frame by frame in *pièce touchée*.
