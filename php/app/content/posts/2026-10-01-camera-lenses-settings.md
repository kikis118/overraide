Booked a Sony FX30 with Samyang 24/45/75mm T1.9 cine primes. Chose it over the Canon C100 and C200 I've used before: it's newer, records 10-bit S-Log3 for heavy grading, handles low light far better, and is small enough to wedge between monitors.

Settings: 4K 25p, 180° shutter, Cine EI on the high base ISO for screen-lit scenes, monitors at 100Hz so their refresh divides evenly into 25fps. In 50Hz mode the camera offers 100fps rather than 120, which conforms to exactly 4× slow motion on a 25p timeline.

A rule for the camera: the human is filmed with a locked camera, and the camera only starts moving once the human has gone.
