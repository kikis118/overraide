Style guide

The rules each shot is checked against.

One source

In the room the monitor is the key. Everything it doesn't reach falls to black. No fill that the room can't explain.

Into purple

The room is cool steel with lilac-grey skin (#5C617A), already leaning towards violet. Through the portal everything turns purple, like the Shaper: indigo shadows, violet cloth, lavender light.

Keep the dark dark

Under-lit CG reads as real and over-lit CG reads as synthetic. The void stays deep indigo, not flat black, with stars in the cloth. One magenta note at most.

Scale through haze

Small silhouette, vast soft-edged form, atmosphere between them. The being is never fully revealed.

Hide the handover

Relight the double to the plate. Match blur, grain and lens distortion. Cut or wipe on movement or an occluder.

Filmed, not rendered

Lens flaws, grain and slight distortion on every CG frame, so the void feels shot on the same camera as the room.

Locked-off plates

Room plates are locked off or simply tracked, which keeps the matchmove and the Gaussian-splat room cheap and stable.

No generated images

Nothing on screen is AI-generated. AI is used only inside the pipeline (roto, denoise) and every use is logged.

Cut to the sound

Cuts into and out of the portal land on the beat. The edit drives the rush.
