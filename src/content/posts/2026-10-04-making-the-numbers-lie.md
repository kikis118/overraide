For the metrics sequence the views need to climb from zero to 150,000 in seconds. Filming a real analytics page for a day was never an option.

Built an After Effects rig instead: a recording of the real page, the numbers removed, and expression-driven counters on top, all controlled by one Progress slider with an adjustable curve. The render plays fullscreen on the monitor and is re-filmed, so it carries real screen light and moiré.

Also scripted a folder of dummy vertical clips that fills itself in Windows Explorer at an accelerating rate: the shorts being dumped, with nobody touching the mouse.
