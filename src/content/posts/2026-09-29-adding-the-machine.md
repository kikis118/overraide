Considered switching to a film about AI in general. Rejected it as a theme on its own: glowing neural nets and Matrix rain are the most crowded imagery there is right now.

Instead I merged the two ideas. I already build tools that cut long streams into shorts automatically. The film becomes an editor teaching a machine to replace his own hand.

Then the layer that explains *why* the hand cuts the way it does: audiences reward negativity. Psychology research finds that bad weighs more than good (Baumeister et al., 2001), and a large study of real headline tests found that negative words measurably increase clicks (Robertson et al., 2023). But it isn't always true, and the film shouldn't pretend it is.

Key word for the film's stance: **ambivalence**.
