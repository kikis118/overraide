The eye close-up wouldn't focus. The cause was minimum focus distance, not technique: the 75mm can't focus close enough. Switched to the Sony 50mm, which focuses at around 30cm and has eye autofocus, stopped down to f/4–5.6 so the whole iris is sharp.

Added aspect ratio as another dial: 2.39:1 at the start, the bars slowly closing through the middle, a switch to vertical 9:16 for the machine's section (the shape everything gets cut into for Shorts), then the bars snapping shut. Precedents: Xavier Dolan's *Mommy* (2014), where a square frame physically widens and closes again, and Abel Gance's three-screen *Napoléon* (1927).
