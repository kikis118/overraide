![Dust in monitor light, with horizontal bands.](/media/dust-frame.jpg)

Shot the dust-in-screen-light close-up at 100fps. Horizontal bands ran through the frame.

**Cause.** At 100fps a 180° shutter exposes for 1/200s. The monitor's light pulses with its refresh and with its backlight dimming, and the sensor reads out line by line, so it records the pulses as stripes. Lower monitor brightness made it worse.

**Fix for the reshoot.** Monitor at full brightness (many monitors stop flickering there), a 360° shutter at 100fps, and checking the preview before rolling.

I may keep some of the banded takes anyway. They read as scan lines, which belong to the machine (Menkman, 2011).

Lighting for the whole shoot: two bike lights, two monitors and the display inside the PC. Nothing else.
