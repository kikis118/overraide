title: "In camera, not in comp"

date: 2026-10-02
summary: "Real content on real screens, and VFX only where it can't be shot."
draft: true
refs: []
---

Decided to put the real content on the monitors and capture it in camera, rather than covering the screens and replacing them in post. The screens are the main light source, so real content gives real light on skin, real reflections in the eye and real moiré. Covering them with green would have spilled green light on everything.

VFX is now limited to what can't be shot: the playhead leaving the monitor (matched with fSpy on a locked-off frame, since a handheld camera track isn't practical on a solo shoot) and the reconstructed room in the last movement.
