A note on motive, written down before I forget how sudden it was.

In August 2026 I started using Claude Code, without realising how powerful it was. Within weeks it had changed how I work entirely. Tasks that took me hours of manual cutting became tools that ran on their own: clipping long streams into shorts, captioning, organising footage.

That is the second half of the film. The first half, the invisible editor shaping what audiences see, has been true for five years. The machine arrived this summer, and it arrived fast. *OVERRAIDE* is partly an attempt to look at that change while I'm still inside it. I don't want the film to decide whether it's good or bad. That's for whoever watches it. The only thing I'm sure of is that it is a change.
